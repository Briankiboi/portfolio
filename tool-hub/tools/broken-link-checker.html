⚙️How it works

Enter a page URL starting with https:// or paste the page’s raw HTML. The tool extracts external links and checks whether they are working, redirected, broken, or blocked. You can export the results as an Excel report.

🔒Privacy

Links are checked directly from your browser, and nothing is stored or tracked. If browser security prevents access, the tool may use a public relay to retrieve the page. For complete privacy, paste the raw HTML instead.

💡Tips
  • Use an https:// URL for the most reliable results.
  • A “Blocked” link may be affected by CORS or a timeout and is not necessarily broken.
  • Paste the raw HTML when the page cannot be fetched or when you want to avoid sending the page through a relay.
