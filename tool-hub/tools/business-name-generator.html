⚙️How it works

Curated word banks of prefixes, suffixes, adjectives and nouns are combined with your industry, chosen style (modern, classic, fun, luxury, short, or all) and an optional keyword to generate fresh name ideas on the spot. Everything is produced locally in your browser — the generator never calls a naming API.

🔒Privacy

Fully offline. Favorites you save are kept only in this device's localStorage and never leave your browser.

💡Tips
  • Type a keyword (e.g. "coffee", "build", "health") to blend it into the combinations.
  • Names are algorithmic inspiration — always check domain availability and trademark registers before registering.
  • Regenerate or switch styles to surface different combinations; keep the ones you like as favorites.
