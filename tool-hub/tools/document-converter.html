🔒Privacy

This is the key detail: your file is never uploaded anywhere. The DOCX is parsed with mammoth and turned into a PDF with html2pdf, all inside this tab. Disconnect from the internet and it still works.

🧩How it works

Your Word file is converted to styled HTML that faithfully keeps paragraphs, headings, lists, bold/italic and images, then rendered page-by-page into a crisp, printable PDF.

💡Tips
  • Large documents may take a few seconds — the work happens in your browser's memory.
  • For best fidelity, keep your formatting in the document body; the PDF follows the visual width of your content.
  • Only content you have the right to reproduce should be converted.
