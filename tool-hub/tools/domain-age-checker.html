Domain Age Checker

Finds the creation date & age of a domain using the authoritative Verisign RDAP registry, plus live DNS records.

Domains & DNS client-side

Result

Enter a domain (e.g. example.com) and click Check. Age + DNS answers load from Verisign RDAP & Google Public DNS.

⚙️How it works

Enter a domain name to retrieve its registry information through RDAP, the modern version of WHOIS. The tool checks the domain’s creation, update, and expiry dates, registrar, nameservers, and age.

It also checks common DNS records such as A, MX, NS, and TXT through Google Public DNS.

🔒Privacy

The tool only sends the domain name you enter to public registry and DNS services. No information is stored or tracked.

💡Tips
  • Domain age is calculated from the official registry creation date.
  • New domains may not have DNS records until they are configured.
  • Missing contact details usually result from registrar privacy protection, not an error.
