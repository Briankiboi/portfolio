Discover the physical city, country, ISP, timezone, and coordinates of any IP address or domain.

Result

Click 📍 Locate to detect your own IP, or 📱 Use My Precise Device Location to pin your current position.

⚙️How it works

📍 Locate resolves an IP, domain or URL (domains are answered by querying Google/Cloudflare's public DNS for the host first) and then geolocates it against a stack of free services tried in order: ipwho.is (primary), ipapi.co and ipinfo.io (automatic backups). Fields are normalised across providers and, when coordinates exist, the result is pinned on an embedded OpenStreetMap. Precise device location instead uses your browser's own GPS/Wi-Fi geolocation to pin where you physically are.

🔒Privacy

Your query (IP or domain) is sent only to the free provider actually used for the lookup — never stored locally, never tracked by this hub.

💡Tips
  • Leave the field empty and click 📍 Locate to detect your own public IP.
  • Provider results are approximate — typically city-level accuracy, not street-level.
  • Precise device location asks for browser permission and generally requires HTTPS or localhost.
