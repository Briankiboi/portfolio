⚙️How it works

Renders your text into a QR code entirely in this page using a lightweight QR library loaded from a CDN. You control the size, the dark/light colors and the error-correction level (L / M / Q / H). The type is auto-detected as you type (URL, phone number or plain text). The finished code can be downloaded as PNG or SVG.

🔒Privacy

Nothing you enter is uploaded or stored — encoding happens 100% on-device. If the CDN library can't load (offline or file://), a notice tells you to serve the folder with python3 -m http.server.

💡Tips
  • A higher error-correction level survives damage or partial obstruction, but makes the code denser.
  • Keep strong dark-on-light contrast for reliable scanning — avoid light dark modules on dark backgrounds.
  • URLs and phone numbers in a QR trigger the scanner's app to open or dial automatically when scanned.
