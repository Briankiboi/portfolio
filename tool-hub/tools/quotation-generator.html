⚙️How it works

You fill in your business details, the customer's info, and the quote's number/date/validity; then add line items (description, quantity, unit price). All arithmetic — subtotal, flat or percentage discount, tax (0 / 5 / 16 / 18%) and grand total — is computed live in your browser with Alpine.js, and the formatted quote preview updates as you type. From there you can print a clean A4 sheet or download it as a PDF, generated fully client-side.

🔒Privacy

Quotation data stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded or saved to any server.

💡Tips
  • Add a validity period (e.g. "Valid for 14 days") to encourage a timely decision.
  • Pick the currency and tax rate that match your jurisdiction before totalling up.
  • Use Download PDF to send a professional file — no account or email needed.
