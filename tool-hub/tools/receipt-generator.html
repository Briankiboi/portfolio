⚙️How it works

Enter your business details and an optional logo (the image is embedded into the document locally), the customer, the receipt number and date, then add line items with quantities and prices. Totals, tax and the final amount are recalculated live in your browser with Alpine.js, and a receipt-style preview updates alongside. Use Download PDF or Print — both generated entirely client-side.

🔒Privacy

Receipt data and your uploaded logo stay in your browser — nothing is uploaded or saved to any server.

💡Tips
  • Leave the receipt number blank to auto-generate one.
  • The preview uses a monospace receipt look; adjust your browser zoom if the PDF page feels off.
  • Choose the tax rate for your jurisdiction and currency before totalling.
