⚙️How it works

Enter an https:// page URL and click Audit Page. The tool fetches the page, analyzes it locally, and checks 18 technical factors related to performance, page structure, media, accessibility, and loading.

You receive:

  • A score out of 100
  • Pass, Warn, and Fail results
  • Practical improvement tips
  • A downloadable CSV report
🔒Privacy

The audit runs in your browser. Only the page HTML is fetched, and nothing is stored or tracked.

💡Tips
  • Use an https:// URL.
  • Fix Fail items first, then address Warn items.
  • Prioritize page size, requests, scripts, images, and loading performance.
  • Use the CSV download to save or share the audit report.
